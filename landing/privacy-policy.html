Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, protect and share your personal and financial information.

Last updated: 17 June 2026

At Innovise Consultant ("Innovise", "we", "us" or "our"), protecting the confidentiality of your information is central to how we work. As a firm of Chartered Accountants and Company Secretaries, we routinely handle sensitive personal, financial and legal documents on your behalf, and we treat that responsibility seriously.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website innovise.in, our client portal, and our services, and how we use and safeguard it. By using our website or engaging our services, you agree to the practices described here.

1.Information We Collect

Depending on the service you request, we may collect the following categories of information:

Information you provide directly

  • Contact details — name, mobile number, email address and postal address, submitted through our contact form, WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Identity and KYC documents — PAN, Aadhaar, passport, voter ID, photographs and similar identity proofs required for registrations and filings.
  • Financial and business information — bank statements, financial records, invoices, GST data, income details, and other records needed for tax, accounting and compliance work.
  • Business and corporate details — company name, proposed objectives, director/partner details, digital signatures (DSC), and related documents.
  • Service queries — any messages, requirements or instructions you share with us.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device information and pages visited, collected through standard website analytics and cookies.

2.How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for legitimate business purposes, including to:

  • Provide the services you have requested — company registration, GST and tax filings, trademark and IPR work, annual compliance, licensing, and related services;
  • Prepare, verify and submit applications, returns and filings to the relevant government authorities;
  • Communicate with you regarding your engagement, share status updates, and respond to queries;
  • Provide quotations, invoices and process payments;
  • Comply with our own legal, regulatory and professional obligations;
  • Improve our website and services.

3.How and When We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only where necessary to deliver our services or where required by law:

  • Government and regulatory authorities — such as the Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA), GST Network, Income Tax Department, Trademark Registry, FSSAI, MSME/Udyam and other bodies, strictly to complete the filings and registrations you have engaged us for.
  • Authorised professionals and partners — qualified CAs, CSs and associates working on your engagement under confidentiality obligations.
  • Service providers — secure third-party tools used for document handling, communication or payments, bound to protect your data.
  • Legal requirement — where disclosure is required by law, court order or a lawful request from an authority.
Your data is never shared with any unrelated third party for marketing or any other purpose without your explicit consent.

4.Data Security

We follow strict confidentiality protocols and apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse. Documents are handled by qualified professionals on a need-to-know basis. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, we work to safeguard your data at every stage of your engagement.

5.Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as needed to provide our services and to meet legal, tax, regulatory and professional record-keeping obligations. Once it is no longer required, we securely delete or anonymise it.

6.Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to ensure the site functions correctly and to understand how visitors use it. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, though some features may not work as intended if you do.

7.Your Rights

In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable Indian law, you may:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • Request deletion of your data, subject to our legal retention obligations;
  • Withdraw consent for non-essential processing, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8.Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external sites, including government portals and our client login at portal.innovise.in. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of websites we do not operate, and we encourage you to read their policies.

9.Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

10.Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or the law. The latest version will always be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
