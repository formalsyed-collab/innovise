GST Return Filing in India: What Every Business Should Know (2026)

Every GST-registered business in India must file periodic returns — reporting sales, purchases, tax collected, and Input Tax Credit (ITC) — with the tax department. This applies even if there's no business activity in a period, in which case a Nil return is still required.

Who Must File

All GST-registered entities, including regular taxpayers, Composition Scheme businesses, e-commerce operators, Input Service Distributors, and non-resident taxable persons.

Key GST Returns

  • GSTR-1 — Reports outward supplies (sales). Filed monthly or quarterly under QRMP.
  • GSTR-3B — Self-declared summary of sales, purchases, tax liability, and ITC claimed; this is where tax is actually paid.
  • GSTR-4 — Annual return for Composition Scheme taxpayers.
  • GSTR-9 — Annual return consolidating the year's filings, mandatory above a turnover threshold.
  • GSTR-9C — Reconciliation statement for businesses above a higher turnover limit.

Late Fees and Consequences

Missing a due date attracts a per-day late fee (CGST and SGST separately) plus interest on outstanding tax. Repeated non-filing can lead to GSTIN suspension, blocking invoicing and e-way bill generation.

Why It Matters

Timely, accurate filing keeps ITC claims dispute-free, builds a clean compliance record for loans and vendor onboarding, and reduces the risk of tax notices.
